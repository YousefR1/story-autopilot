Privacy Policy

Story Autopilot · Last updated November 1, 2026

Story Autopilot is a personal tool operated by the owner of the channel Your Channel Name. It creates short story videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It has no other users.

What data the app accesses

  • TikTok: basic profile information (to confirm which account is connected) and permission to upload videos to that account.
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the owner's channel.

How it is used

Access is used only to upload videos the owner's own automation has produced. The app does not read, collect, sell or share data about viewers or any other people.

Storage and security

Login tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's GitHub account and are used only during the daily upload. No personal data is stored anywhere else.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services) and in your Google Account (Security › Third-party access).
